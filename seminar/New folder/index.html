About the seminar

Mathematics in conversation.

The Mathematics Seminar brings together researchers and students from across pure and applied mathematics. Speakers are encouraged to make their talks accessible beyond a narrow specialty while still giving a genuine view of the ideas that drive their work.

Everyone is welcome. No registration is needed, and coffee is served before each talk.
