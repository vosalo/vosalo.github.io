About the seminar

Symbolic dynamics seminar.

We are organizing a symbolic dynamics research seminar. The plan is to meet every Wednesday at 14 in QM3 (when it's available!), and alternate speakers among those interested. You can email the organizers (e.g. me or Ilkka) if you have ideas. Watch this website for updates. Everyone is extremely welcome to join. Feel free to be an active part of shaping the seminar, or just come and lurk!
