Draw a polygon C by clicking on the grid. Draw counterclockwise and make sure it's convex -- otherwise, it won't work. You can draw a discrete shape on the grid at the top left corner.

Press 'e' to erase the polygon.

Press 'x' to draw the polygon D that's determined by the shape and C so that the contents of C \setminus D can be chosen arbitrarily and uniquely determine the contents of D. Press 'y' to get an alert telling you the number of ways to fill the polygon in a TEP subshift -- if your shape is Ledrappier and the polygon is small enough, this will also be checked by brute force for the Ledrappier subshift.
